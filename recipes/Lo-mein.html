Lo Mein Noodles Recipes

These lo mein noodles were created from a blend of multiple recipes. Add your favorite meat for a main dish or serve as a side. If serving with meat, cook the meat separately. The recipes you found in this page are source from this website allrecipes

Ingredients List

  • 1 (8 ounce) package spaghetti
  • 3 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons teriyaki sauce
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 1/4 teaspoons ground ginger
  • 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil
  • 3 stalks celery, sliced
  • 2 large carrots
  • 1/2 sweet onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 green onions, sliced plus more for garnish

Direction of Cooking

  1. Step 1

    2. Gather all your ingredients.

  2. Step 2

    3. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook spaghetti in boiling water, stirring occasionally, until tender yet firm to the bite, about 12 minutes; drain, then rinse with cold water to cool.

  3. Step 3

    4. Meanwhile, whisk soy sauce, teriyaki sauce, honey, and ginger together in a small bowl; set aside.

  4. Step 4

    5. While the spaghetti cooks, heat oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat. Cook and stir celery, carrots, onion, and green onions until slightly tender, 5 to 7 minutes.

  5. Step 5

    6. Add spaghetti and soy sauce mixture. Cook, stirring frequently, until heated through and evenly coated, about 2 to 3 minutes. Serve garnished with additional sliced green onions, if desired.
